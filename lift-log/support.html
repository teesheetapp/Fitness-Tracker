WORK: Lift Log

Getting started

After you create an account, pick a starting program (Full Body, Push / Pull / Legs, or Upper / Lower). It sets up an 8-week plan beginning this week. Change any exercise, set, or day under Workouts and Phases.

Logging a workout

On Today, tap Log This Workout. Last time's numbers appear in the corner of each box, and the +/− buttons adjust the set you're on. Check off a set to start its rest timer. Tap Save Session when you're done.

Units

Switch between lb and kg in Settings → Units. Numbers you already logged aren't converted.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and use the code we send to choose a new one.

Back up or delete your data

Settings → Your data → Export saves a full copy. Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and everything in it.

Contact

Testers: send questions and bug reports to the person who invited you. A support email will be listed here before public release.
