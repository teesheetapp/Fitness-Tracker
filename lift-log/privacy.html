Privacy Policy

WORK: Lift Log · Last updated November 5, 2026

WORK: Lift Log is a workout log. This policy explains what the app stores about you, why, and how to remove it.

Short version: we store your email address and the workouts you enter so your log syncs between devices. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we can't read them.
  • Your training data: workouts, programs, logged sets (weight, reps, RPE), notes, personal records, bodyweight entries, rest days, and your settings such as lb or kg.
  • A copy on your phone: the app keeps your data on your device so it works without a connection.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, health records, advertising identifiers, or analytics that follow you outside the app. If the app later offers Apple Health, it will only read or write the data you approve in iOS, and this policy will be updated first.

How it's used

Only to run the app: signing you in, syncing your log, and showing your history, records, and charts. We use your email to send sign-up codes and password reset codes. We don't send marketing email.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in the United States. Each account can only read its own data; this is enforced by the database itself.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data and don't share it with advertisers. It is shared only with the service providers that run the app (Supabase, and our email provider for sign-up and reset codes), or if required by law.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Your data → Export saves a full copy.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and all of its data from our servers and from that phone. This can't be undone.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: support@workliftlog.com.
